A—03 How a project runs

Five steps, one point of contact

01

Consultation

We walk the space, hear what's not working, and give you a straight read on scope and rough budget before anything is signed.

02

Pre-design & scope

Measurements, structural checks, and a defined scope of work — the groundwork that keeps the design phase from stalling later.

03

Design & permitting

Layouts, material selections, and drawings finalized, then filed with your city for permit before any demo starts.

04

Build

Our crew builds the project with a fixed project manager, so you're never re-explaining the plan to someone new.

05

Walkthrough & warranty

A final walkthrough against the original scope, and we stay reachable for repairs long after the project wraps.
